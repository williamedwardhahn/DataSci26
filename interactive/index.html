Mathematics of Data Science · Fall 2026

Nine things you can
only learn by moving them.

Each plate is a single page that runs entirely in your browser — no install, no account, nothing uploaded. Open one alongside the lab notebook for that week and change every number until the picture stops surprising you.

Weeks 7 — 8 · Images
  • Plate 01

    Image Basics

    Brightness, contrast, gamma, blur and bit depth — with the histogram moving underneath.

  • Plate 02

    Bit Depth & Dithering

    Quantise to one bit and watch dithering buy back the detail. PSNR included.

  • Plate 03

    Colormaps

    Why viridis and not jet, argued with a lightness curve rather than an opinion.

  • Plate 04

    Gamma & Tone Mapping

    The transfer curve between what a sensor recorded and what a screen shows.

  • Plate 05

    Image Combinatorics

    Count every possible image. Compare it to the atoms in the universe. Keep going.

    Convolution

  • Plate 08

  • Plate 09
